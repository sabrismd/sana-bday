the last page · for now

Oru kadidham, climax-la

Thangameyyyy,

Ippo indha site-oda last page-ku vandhuttom. Aana idhukkulla evlo vishayangal irukku theriyuma? Namma kadhaiya sollura oru chinna book, namma memories-ah appadiye freeze panni vechurukkura moments, un songs-kaana oru chinna concert hall, un feelings ovvonnukkum oru jar full-ah duas, unakkaaga ezhudhunna letters, namma future dreams, appuram twenty candles-ku badhila unakkaaga irubadhu duas. Idhellam serndhu, namma rendu perukkum mattum puriyura oru chinna ulagam maadhiri irukku. 🤍

Edhukku idhellam pannen theriyuma? Romba simple, dii. Nee enna miss panra nerathula, midnight aagirundhaalum, un mood off-ah irundhaalum, illa ennaala udane reply panna mudiyama ponaalum, nee inga thirumbi varalaam. Indha page-ah open panni, namma memories-ah paakkalaam, un favourite songs-ah kekkalaam, unakkaaga naan ezhudhi vecha words-ah padikkalaam. Naan pakkathula illaadha nerathulayum, en paasam unakku konjamavadhu feel aaganum nu dhaan idhellam panninen. Nee inga varumbodhellam, en voice un kooda irukkura maadhiri feel aaganum, qalbii. 🫂

Namma perfect illa. Sandaiyum vandhirukku, misunderstandings-um irundhirukku, sila neram pesama irundha moments-um irundhirukku. Aana ellathukkum naduvula, namma bond-ah purinjukka, orutharoda oruthar feelings-ah ketka, thirumba thirumba orutharai oruthar choose panna try pannirukkom. Enakku adhu dhaan love-la romba azhagaana vishayam. Love panren nu solradhu mattum illa, kashtamaana nerathulayum care pannradhu, purinjukka muyarchi panradhu, thappu panninaa adha othukkuradhu, serndhu better-ah valar radhu — idhellam dhaan oru bonding-ah special aakkudhu. ❤️

Un twentieth year ippadi azhaga aarambichirukkuradhai paakkumbodhu, oru vishayam sollanum nu thonudhu. Birthday unakku dhaan, aana gift kidaichadhu enakkum dhaan — because en life-la nee irukka. Allah unna en vazhkaila oru azhagaana ni'mah-ah thandhirukkaan nu nenachaa, enakku manasula happiness dhaan varudhu. Unna naan sandhichadhu, unna therinjukittadhu, un kooda indha memories ellam create panradhu — idhellathukkum Alhamdulillah. 🤲🏻

Namma future eppadi irukkum nu enakku ella details-um theriyadhu, thangam. Aana namma rendu perukkum nalladhu nadakkanum, namma bond-la love-um rahmah-vum irukkanum, Allah namma vazhiya barakah-oda nirappanum nu manasaara dua panren. Nee en kooda irukkura ovvoru naalum special dhaan. Namma kanavugal ellam nalla nerathula, nalla vazhiyila niraiveranum. In Sha Allah. 🤍

So, kadaisiya namma style-la sollanum-na… Lubb you so much, Sana Thangameyy. Habun u — innikku mattum illa, ovvoru naalum, un ovvoru mood-layum, Allah namakkaaga ezhudhura ovvoru pudhu chapter-layum. Namma rendu perukkum nalladhu edhuvo, adhai Allah namakku azhagaana vazhiyila tharanum. Ameen. 🤲🏻❤️
